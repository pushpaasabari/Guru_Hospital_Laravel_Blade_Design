# Guru Hospital Discharge Summary – First Page Skill

## 1. Purpose

Create a professional, clinically accurate first page of a Guru Hospital Discharge Summary, compatible with the hospital's official pre-printed letterhead.

## 2. Mandatory Page Configuration

Always configure the document with these exact custom page dimensions:

- Page width: 223 mm
- Page height: 304 mm
- Orientation: Portrait
- Top margin: 70 mm
- Bottom margin: 30 mm
- Left and right margins: Preserve the approved existing letterhead layout.
- Printable content height: 204 mm.

These dimensions are mandatory. Never substitute A4, Legal, Letter, or other standard page sizes.

## 3. Pre-Printed Letterhead Rules

The output is intended for printing on the official Guru Hospital pre-printed letterhead.

- Do not reproduce the hospital logo.
- Do not duplicate the hospital name or address.
- Do not generate a header or footer already printed on the letterhead.
- Do not place any text, lines, tables, or other content inside the 70 mm top blank area.
- Do not place any content inside the 30 mm bottom blank area.
- Ensure all clinical content is positioned within the available printable region.

## 4. First Page Content

Organize the first page using the following sections, as applicable:

1. DISCHARGE SUMMARY title.
2. Patient identification:
   - Patient name
   - Age and sex
   - UHID / IP number
   - Date of admission
   - Date of discharge
   - Treating consultant
3. Clinical information:
   - Chief complaints
   - History of present illness
   - Relevant past medical and surgical history
   - Relevant examination findings
   - Provisional and final diagnosis
4. Investigations and significant findings.
5. Treatment and hospital course.
6. Procedure / surgery details, if applicable.
7. Condition at discharge.

Preserve all relevant clinical information from the source document.

## 5. Formatting Standards

- Use a clean, professional hospital-document design.
- Use consistent typography and spacing.
- Use bold section headings.
- Use structured tables for patient information where appropriate.
- Maintain clear alignment and readable font sizes.
- Avoid excessive decorative elements.
- Prevent overlapping, clipping, and broken text.
- Avoid unnecessary whitespace within the printable area.

## 6. Clinical Accuracy Rules

- Never invent patient details, diagnoses, investigation results, medication details, or treatment information.
- Do not change clinical meaning while correcting grammar.
- Preserve dates, dosages, units, and investigation values accurately.
- Retain clinically significant positive and negative findings.
- Mark unavailable information as blank or not documented rather than guessing.
- Flag contradictory clinical information for verification.

## 7. Page Overflow Rules

- First attempt to fit the content through professional spacing optimization.
- Adjust paragraph spacing and table cell padding before reducing font size.
- Do not violate the 70 mm top and 30 mm bottom blank areas.
- Do not shrink the entire document excessively.
- If content cannot fit legibly, continue onto the next page rather than deleting important clinical information.

## 8. Output Validation

Before finalizing, verify:

- Page dimensions are exactly 223 × 304 mm.
- Top blank area is exactly 70 mm.
- Bottom blank area is exactly 30 mm.
- No duplicated hospital branding.
- No missing clinical information.
- All text is readable.
- No content is clipped or overlapping.
- PDF prints correctly on the official pre-printed letterhead.

## 9. Final Deliverable

Produce a print-ready PDF of the first discharge summary page, with the original clinical information preserved and the official pre-printed letterhead layout respected.
